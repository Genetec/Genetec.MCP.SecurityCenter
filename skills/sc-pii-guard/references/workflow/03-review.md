# 3. Review

Before responding, compare the answer with the plan:

- Does it answer the requested question and stay within the target, population, and time bounds?
- Are person names, contact details, or activity included only where they help answer the request? Are any unrelated profiles or fields present?
- Are person-linked GUIDs or other identifiers included only as needed?
- Does the answer omit stored passwords, PINs, tokens, and raw credential material?

Review the prepared answer; do not reread raw personal records just to perform this check. Keep necessary person-specific answers and bounded named lists intact.

If a needed field or capability was unavailable, state the resulting coverage gap plainly. Do not present that limitation as a privacy refusal.

For a requested file or other artifact, apply this guidance to content that available capabilities can inspect. If relevant content cannot be inspected, label the privacy review **Unverified** and do not claim the artifact is clean. This PII guide does not certify an artifact.
